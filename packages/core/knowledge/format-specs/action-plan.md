# Action Plan: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A short plan that turns one problem or goal into dated, funded actions. A teacher uses it for a learning area plan, a class intervention or a small school project. It is signed by the teacher and approved by the school head.

## Page setup
A4 landscape, 1-inch margins, Arial 12 (table cells may use Arial 11). One table, one page wide. Repeat the header row on each page.

## Parts, in order
Layout A (teacher plan, default):

| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header: Title, School, School Year | Plan title, school name, SY like 2026-2027 | Profile/letterhead (school, SY); teacher (title) | One line each |
| Activities | The actions to be done | AI drafts from teacher's problem; teacher approves | 1-3 short phrases per objective |
| Objectives | What each activity is meant to achieve | AI drafts; teacher approves | One SMART sentence per row |
| Timeline | Start and end dates or months | Teacher | Real dates inside the school year |
| Person/s Responsible | Roles, then names the teacher gave | Teacher | Roles first |
| Resources Needed | Items, quantities, funds if any | Teacher | Short phrases |
| Success Indicators / Means of Verification | Checkable result and the proof | AI drafts; teacher approves | One measurable phrase plus the proof |
| Prepared by | Teacher name and position | Profile; signature left blank | Blank line |
| Approved | School Principal | Profile; signature left blank | Blank line |

Layout B (school-level plan, only if the teacher asks): header Name of School, Classification, District, Division, Region; a GOAL line; then the table Objectives | Action Steps | Resources Needed (Human, Financial, Material) | Date and Duration | Evidence of Success/Improvement.

## How to fill each part
- Title: area plus school year. Good: "Grade 5 Science Remediation Plan SY 2026-2027". Bad: "Plan" (names nothing).
- Activities: concrete verb phrases. Good: "Hold a 20-minute remedial session every Wednesday". Bad: "Various activities as needed" (cannot be checked).
- Objectives: verb + what + baseline to target + by when. Good: "Raise the Grade 5 Science MPS from 68 to 75 by the end of Term 3." Bad: "Improve performance." If the teacher gave no baseline, write the objective without numbers and leave the number blank.
- Timeline: start and end inside the school year. Bad: "Whole year" on every row.
- Person/s Responsible: "Subject teacher; Learning Area Coordinator". Bad: assigning a person the teacher never named.
- Resources Needed: "Bond paper, 2 reams; printed drill sheets". Leave empty if none.
- Success Indicators: "85% of learners score at least 75% in the Term 3 test; attendance sheets". Bad: "Learners improved."

## Writing rules
- Plain English; Filipino only if the teacher writes in Filipino.
- Cells are phrases, not paragraphs. Future tense for activities.
- Every objective is Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, Time-bound.
- Avoid: "various", "as needed", "etc.", "improve", "enhance" with no number.

## Free checks code can run
- Every row has an activity, an objective, a timeline and an indicator.
- Start date is on or before end date; both fall inside the school year.
- Money cells are numbers; each amount has a funding source; a total is computed by code.
- No two rows have identical objectives.
- Prepared by and Approved lines are present and blank.

## AI must never write
- Names of people, baselines, targets, amounts, funding sources or dates the teacher did not give.
- Approval, signature or "Approved" status.
- Success claims or results. Leave the cell empty and flag it for the teacher.

## Sample (fictional, for style only)
**Grade 5 Science Remediation Plan SY 2026-2027**
School: Sample Elementary School

| Activities | Objectives | Timeline | Person/s Responsible | Resources Needed | Success Indicators / Means of Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Give a diagnostic test on Term 1 topics | Identify learners below 75% in Term 1 Science by August 28, 2026 | Aug 17 - Aug 28, 2026 | Teacher A | Printed test, 40 copies | List of learners below 75%; test results sheet |
| Hold a 20-minute remedial session every Wednesday | Raise the Grade 5 Science MPS from 68 to 75 by the end of Term 3 | Sep 2, 2026 - Feb 24, 2027 | Teacher A; Learning Area Coordinator | Drill sheets; bond paper, 2 reams | 85% of learners score at least 75% in the Term 3 test; attendance sheets |
| Send a progress note to parents each term | Inform parents of every remedial learner's progress by the end of each term | Oct 30, 2026; Jan 29, 2027 | Teacher A; class adviser | Progress note forms | Signed acknowledgement slips |

Prepared by: Teacher A, Teacher III ______________
Approved: ______________, School Principal

## Open points
- Layout A or B as the default is a house choice; no official template exists.
- The existing runtime guide uses a 15-column spreadsheet with budget and source columns; this spec drops them unless the teacher gives a budget. Owner to confirm.
- DO 015 s.2026 may change "Quarter" to "Term"; this spec uses Term.
