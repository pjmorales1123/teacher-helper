# Action Research Proposal: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A short plan for a classroom action research study, filed with the school head and the division or regional research committee (for example a BERF call). The teacher states a problem in their own class, the change they will try, and how they will check if it worked. It is a proposal, so it holds no findings.

## Page setup
A4 portrait, 1-inch margins, Arial 12, single spacing with a blank line between parts. A title page and a table of contents are added when the call asks for them (RRC/BERF version). Some calls cap the length (about 3,500 words, unverified): follow the call.

## Parts, in order

| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title page | Title, proponent, school, division, committee and call name, school year | Profile; teacher | One page; blanks for the teacher |
| Proponent form (if the call asks) | Name, position, school address, contact, attainment | Profile | Blank lines; never invent |
| Context and Rationale | The problem in the class, why it matters, supporting data, related studies | Teacher facts; AI organizes | 2-4 short paragraphs |
| Action Research Questions | The main question and 2-3 sub-questions | AI drafts; teacher approves | Questions, one line each |
| Proposed Innovation, Intervention and Strategy | What will be tried, how often, for how long | AI drafts from teacher notes | One paragraph or a short list |
| Action Research Methods: Participants and/or Other Sources of Data and Information | Who and what data sources | Teacher | Counts only if given |
| Methods: Data Gathering Methods | Tools and when used | AI suggests; teacher approves | Short list |
| Methods: Data Analysis Plan | How data will be compared | AI suggests | 2-3 sentences |
| Action Research Work Plan and Timelines | Table: Activity, Date, Person Responsible | Teacher dates; AI activity names | 5-8 rows, dates in order |
| Cost Estimates | Table: Item, Quantity, Unit Cost, Total | Teacher | Totals by code |
| Plans for Dissemination and Utilization | How results will be shared and used | AI drafts | 2-3 sentences |
| References | Real sources only | Teacher; never invented | APA style; empty if none given |
| Endorsement and declarations | Supervisor conforme, anti-plagiarism and conflict-of-interest statements | Profile; signatures blank | Blank lines |

## How to fill each part
- **Context and Rationale.** Start with what the teacher sees in class, add the data the teacher gave, then why it matters. Good: "In my Grade 8 class, 12 of 40 learners scored below 75 in the last two periodical tests." Bad: "Many students are struggling" with no number.
- **Research questions.** Each question is answerable with the planned data. Good: "Does peer tutoring raise Grade 8 quiz scores in Algebra?" Bad: "How can education be improved?"
- **Intervention.** Say what, how often, how long, who does it. Good: "Twice-weekly 20-minute peer tutoring for eight weeks." Bad: "Various strategies."
- **Methods.** Tie each question to one data source (quiz scores, checklist, interview). Never state a sample size the teacher did not give.
- **Work plan.** Verbs first: "Administer pre-test." Dates inside the school year and in order.
- **Costs.** Itemize. Good: "Bond paper, 2 reams". Zero-cost items are written as 0, not skipped.
- **Dissemination.** Name the audience (LAC, faculty meeting, division forum), not "everyone."

## Writing rules
- Plain English, future tense for the plan, present tense for the problem.
- One idea per sentence. No findings, no claims that the intervention works.
- Every question links to a data source; every data source answers a question.
- References are real or left empty. No invented authors.

## Free checks code can run
- All eight DepEd parts present with text.
- Every research question has a matching data source in Methods.
- Work plan dates are in order and inside the school year.
- Cost totals equal quantity x unit cost, and the grand total equals the sum.
- References list is empty or each entry has author, year, title.
- No blank part, no "TBD", no bracket placeholders.

## AI must never write
- Baseline scores, learner counts, names, test results or any data the teacher did not give.
- Findings or conclusions.
- Costs, dates, signatures, approvals, the supervisor's conforme.
- Citations that were not supplied.

## Sample (fictional, for style only)
**Title:** Peer Tutoring to Improve Grade 8 Algebra Quiz Scores at Sample National High School
**Proponent:** Teacher A, Sample National High School, Sample Division. School Year 2026-2027.

**Context and Rationale.** In my Grade 8 Mathematics classes, 12 of 40 learners scored below 75 in the first two Algebra quizzes this term. These learners often finish tasks late and seldom ask questions in class. Peer tutoring lets classmates explain steps in simple words, so it may help.

**Action Research Questions.** (1) Does twice-weekly peer tutoring raise Algebra quiz scores of Grade 8 learners? (2) How do tutored learners describe the sessions?

**Proposed Innovation, Intervention and Strategy.** Pair each learner who scored below 75 with a classmate who scored 85 or higher. Pairs meet twice a week for 20 minutes for eight weeks, using the same practice sheets.

**Methods.** Participants: Grade 8 learners of one section. Data gathering: Quiz 1 and Quiz 2 scores before; Quiz 3 to 6 scores during; a five-item feedback form at the end. Data analysis: compare average quiz scores before and after; list common feedback answers.

**Work Plan and Timelines.**

| Activity | Date | Person Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| Form pairs | 2026-08-17 | Teacher A |
| Start sessions | 2026-08-24 | Teacher A |
| Mid-point quiz review | 2026-09-21 | Teacher A |
| End sessions and feedback form | 2026-10-16 | Teacher A |
| Analyze data and write report | 2026-10-30 | Teacher A |

**Cost Estimates.**

| Item | Quantity | Unit Cost | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bond paper (practice sheets) | 2 reams | 250 | 500 |
| Photocopying | 1 lot | 300 | 300 |
| **Total** | | | **800** |

**Dissemination and Utilization.** Results will be shared at the Mathematics LAC and the faculty meeting, and the practice sheets will be offered to other Grade 8 teachers.

**References.** (none supplied)
