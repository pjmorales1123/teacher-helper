# Activity Proposal: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A request to hold one school, district or division activity, such as a contest, orientation, exhibit or sports event. The teacher gives the purpose, plan and cost so the school head can approve it. It is not a training program design.

## Page setup
A4 portrait, 1-inch margins, Arial 12. Budget table is full width. Two to three pages.

## Parts, in order

| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level of Activity | School, District or Division (tick one) | Teacher | One tick |
| Proponents | Who proposes | Profile (teacher, school); teacher adds others | Names and positions, one per line |
| Title | Name of the activity | Teacher | One line |
| Type of activity | Tick: General Assembly, Media-related, Launching/Kick-off, Meeting/Orientation, Contest/Competition, Publicity/Awareness, Exhibit, Sports/Tournament, Others | Teacher | One tick; if Others, name it |
| Purpose | Why the activity is held, in one line | AI drafts; teacher approves | 1 sentence |
| Rationale | Need, data, link to a plan or policy | Teacher facts; AI organizes | One short paragraph |
| Objectives | What the activity will achieve | AI drafts; teacher approves | 2-4 measurable bullets |
| Expected Output | What exists when it ends | AI drafts; teacher approves | 2-4 countable items |
| Information: Date | Date or date range | Teacher | Full date |
| Information: Time | Start and end time | Teacher | e.g. 8:00 AM - 12:00 NN |
| Information: Venue | Place | Teacher | One line |
| Information: Budget allocation | Total cost and source | Teacher; total by code | Pesos and fund source |
| Information: Target participants and count | Who attends and how many | Teacher | Group and number |
| Information: Resource persons / TWG | Speakers or technical working group | Teacher | Roles; names only if given |
| Budget table | Items, Quantity, Unit cost, Amount, Total | Teacher; totals by code | One row per item |
| Prepared by / Noted by / Approved | Signature lines | Profile for typed positions; names left blank | Blank lines |

## How to fill each part
- Title: Good: "Science Quiz Bee 2026, Grade 5". Bad: "Activity".
- Purpose: Good: "To give Grade 5 learners a fun way to review Term 1 Science." Bad: "To do the activity."
- Rationale: need, then the teacher's data, then the plan or policy. Good: "Term 1 Science MPS in Grade 5 was 68%; a quiz bee supports the school's reading and numeracy goals." Bad: invented percentages.
- Objectives: Good: "At the end of the activity, 30 learners will have answered review questions on Term 1 topics." Bad: "To hold a quiz bee" (an activity, not an objective).
- Expected Output: Good: "30 participants; 1 score sheet; 3 winners recognised." Bad: "Success."
- Budget table: item, quantity and unit cost are the teacher's. Bad: round guesses with no quantity.

## Writing rules
- Formal, plain English; future tense (it is a proposal).
- One idea per bullet; no placeholders such as "[specify]".
- Objectives and outputs must match each other.
- Avoid: "etc.", "and others", "various", "hoping to".

## Free checks code can run
- One level and one activity type are ticked.
- Date is inside the school year; time end is after time start.
- Each budget row: Amount equals Quantity times Unit cost; Total equals the sum; Budget allocation equals Total.
- A non-zero total has a fund source.
- Participant count is a number; objectives count is 2-4.
- Signature lines present and blank.

## AI must never write
- Dates, times, venues, counts, amounts, fund sources or resource-person names the teacher did not give.
- Signatories, approvals, or "approved" wording.
- Data in the Rationale that the teacher did not supply.

## Sample (fictional, for style only)
**Level of Activity:** School
**Proponents:** Teacher A, Teacher III, Sample Elementary School
**Title:** Science Quiz Bee 2026, Grade 5
**Type of activity:** Contest/Competition
**Purpose:** To give Grade 5 learners a fun way to review Term 1 Science.
**Rationale:** The Grade 5 Term 1 Science MPS was 68%. A quiz bee gives learners a low-pressure review and supports the school's learning recovery goal.
**Objectives:**
- Review Term 1 Science topics with all 30 participants.
- Recognise the top three learners.
**Expected Output:** 30 participants; 1 score sheet; 3 winners recognised.
