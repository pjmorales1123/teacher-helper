# Counseling Referral Form: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A short note a teacher sends to the school counselor or guidance designate when a learner shows signs of needing help. It records what was observed, not why. The form carries a data privacy notice and is confidential.

## Page setup
A4, portrait, Arial 12, 1-inch margins. Header fields in a two-column table, then the behaviour checklist, then the reason text.

## Parts, in order
| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| COUNSELING REFERRAL FORM | Fixed title | Code | One line |
| Date | Date of referral | Teacher | One line |
| School | School name | Profile/letterhead | One line |
| School Year | For example 2026-2027 | Profile | One line |
| Name of Learner | Learner name | Blank | Line empty |
| Grade & Section | Grade and section | Blank or teacher | One line |
| Name of Parent/Guardian | Parent or guardian | Blank | Line empty |
| Contact Number (parent) | Number | Blank | Line empty |
| Source of Referral, Designation | Referring teacher and position | Profile | One line |
| Contact Number (source) | Teacher number | Profile | One line |
| Did the learner agree to be referred? | Yes / No tick | Teacher | Tick box |
| Reason for Referral, A. Behaviours checklist | Ticked observed behaviours, plus Others | Teacher ticks | Tick boxes |
| Reason for Referral, written reason | Observed facts that led to the referral | Teacher facts, AI wording | 3 to 6 sentences |
| Initial actions taken | What the teacher already did | Teacher facts, AI wording | Short list |
| Privacy notice | Fixed notice | Code | Small print |

## How to fill each part
- **Learner identity.** Name, parent and contact lines stay blank. The teacher fills them by hand. The AI never fills them.
- **Did the learner agree.** Teacher ticks. If the teacher did not say, leave both boxes empty.
- **Behaviours checklist.** Tick only what the teacher said. Never tick self-harm, suicide, abuse or bullying unless the teacher stated it. Those need immediate action by the school, so say "tell the School Head now" in the note to the teacher, not in the form.
- **Written reason.** Observed behaviour with dates and how often. Good: "Late 6 times in October. Sits alone at recess since the second week." Bad: "Probably has problems at home." (guess about cause)
- **Initial actions.** Good: "Talked with the learner privately on October 14." Bad: "Tried everything."

## Writing rules
- Observed behaviour only, in plain words. Past tense.
- English or Filipino as the teacher wrote.
- No diagnosis, no cause, no label on the learner.
- Respect the learner: no quotes that shame.
- Avoid: "depressed", "troubled", "hopeless", "problem child".

## Free checks code can run
- Date and School Year are filled; date is not in the future.
- Source of referral and designation are filled.
- Every ticked behaviour is one the teacher supplied.
- Written reason names at least one observed behaviour or is marked "to add".
- Learner, parent and contact lines are blank.
- Privacy notice is present.

## AI must never write
Learner or parent names, ages, diagnoses, causes, blame, statements the learner did not say, dates, counts of absences or lates, attendance, grades, signatories, or ticks the teacher did not give. Leave the line blank for the teacher.
