# Home Visitation Form: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A record of a teacher's visit to a learner's home, usually after repeated absences or a concern that needs the family. It lists why the teacher went and what was agreed. The form carries a data privacy notice and is kept confidential.

## Page setup
A4, portrait, Arial 12, 1-inch margins. A header table for learner and parent lines, two text boxes, then signature lines.

## Parts, in order
| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOME VISITATION FORM | Fixed title | Code | One line |
| Name of Learner | Learner name | Blank | Line empty |
| LRN | Learner number | Blank | Line empty |
| Level | Grade level | Blank or teacher | One line |
| Address | Home address | Blank | Line empty |
| Birthday | Date | Blank | Line empty |
| Gender | Sex | Blank | Line empty |
| Age | Number | Blank | Line empty |
| Name of Parent/Guardian | Name | Blank | Line empty |
| Learner's Contact Number | Number | Blank | Line empty |
| Parent/Guardian Contact Number | Number | Blank | Line empty |
| REASON FOR HOME VISITATION | Why the visit happened | Teacher facts, AI wording | 2 to 4 sentences |
| REMARKS / AGREEMENT | What was seen, said and agreed | Teacher facts, AI wording | 3 to 6 sentences or bullets |
| Learner's Signature Over Printed Name | Signature | Blank | Line empty |
| Parent's/Guardian Signature Over Printed Name | Signature | Blank | Line empty |
| Prepared by | Teacher name and position (Adviser) | Profile, signature blank | One line |
| Privacy notice | Fixed notice | Code | Small print |

## How to fill each part
- **Learner and parent lines.** Learner identity stays blank. The teacher writes them by hand. LRN, address and birthday are never asked for or written by the AI.
- **Reason for home visitation.** State the cause of the visit as facts. Good: "The learner was absent 8 school days in October and the family could not be reached by phone." Bad: "The family does not care about school." (judgment)
- **Remarks / Agreement.** What the teacher saw and heard, then what both sides agreed. Good: "The parent said the learner helps at the stall in the morning. Agreed: the learner will attend from Monday and the teacher will send modules." Bad: "Parent was uncooperative."
- **Signatures.** Always blank.

## Writing rules
- Respectful, neutral, past tense. English or Filipino as the teacher wrote.
- Describe the home only as far as the teacher needs. No remarks on poverty, appearance or family life.
- Agreements are things both sides said, not teacher wishes.
- Avoid: "neglect", "irresponsible", "poor family", "broken home".

## Free checks code can run
- Reason and Remarks are not empty.
- Date of visit (if the teacher gives one) is not in the future.
- At least one agreement line exists, or the text says "no agreement reached".
- All learner, parent and signature lines are blank.
- Privacy notice is present.

## AI must never write
Names, LRN, addresses, birthdays, ages, home conditions the teacher did not describe, reasons for absence, causes, blame, statements the parent did not give, dates, counts, attendance, promises, signatories. Leave the line blank for the teacher.
