# Minutes of HPTA Meeting: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
The written record of a Homeroom or School PTA meeting. A teacher acting as secretary writes what was discussed and agreed. The minutes are read and approved at the next meeting.

## Page setup
A4, portrait, Arial 12, 1-inch margins. Issues, suggestions and agreements go in a three-column table.

## Parts, in order
| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minutes of HPTA Meeting | Fixed title | Code | One line |
| School and grade/section | Where the meeting belongs | Profile | One line |
| Date | Meeting date | Teacher | One line |
| Time | Start time | Teacher | One line |
| Venue (optional) | Place | Teacher | One line |
| Agenda | Topics in the order set | Teacher | Numbered list |
| Attendees (attendance and quorum) | Counts of parents and teachers present | Teacher | Counts and a quorum line |
| Proceedings | What was said, in agenda order | Teacher notes, AI wording | 1 short paragraph per item |
| Issues and Concerns (table column) | Problem raised | Teacher | One row each |
| Suggestions (table column) | What was suggested | Teacher | One row each |
| Agreement (table column) | What was decided | Teacher | One row each |
| Adjournment | Time the meeting ended | Teacher | One line |
| Prepared by | Secretary name and position | Teacher, blank | Line empty |
| Noted by (optional) | School Head or HPTA president | Blank | Line empty |

## How to fill each part
- **Date, time, venue.** Exact. Good: "October 25, 2026, 3:00 PM, Grade 3 room".
- **Agenda.** Number each item. Good: "1. Funds for the Science Fair. 2. Attendance in October."
- **Attendees.** Counts only if the teacher gave them. Good: "27 of 40 parents present; quorum met." Bad: "Most parents came." Names are not listed unless the teacher gave a list.
- **Proceedings.** What was said and by whom (by role). Good: "The adviser reported 5 absences in the week. A parent asked for a text reminder." Bad: "The parents were happy with everything."
- **Table.** One row per issue, with the matching suggestion and agreement. If nothing was agreed, write "No agreement yet".
- **Adjournment.** Time stated by the teacher.

## Writing rules
- Past tense, neutral, third person. Say "the adviser reported", not "I said".
- English or Filipino as the teacher wrote.
- Record votes and amounts exactly as given.
- Avoid: "unanimously" unless the teacher said so; praise; personal opinion.

## Free checks code can run
- Date, time and adjournment time are filled; adjournment is after start.
- Attendee counts are numbers; present count is not above the stated total.
- Quorum line matches the numbers if a rule was given.
- Each agenda item has a Proceedings entry.
- Each table row has an Agreement or "No agreement yet".
- Prepared by and Noted by lines are blank.

## AI must never write
Names of attendees or speakers, counts, quorum, attendance, votes, amounts, resolutions, dates, times, statements not given, signatories. Leave the line blank for the teacher.

## Sample (fictional, for style only)
**Minutes of HPTA Meeting**

School: Sample Elementary School, Grade 3 - Sample Section
Date: October 25, 2026
Time: 3:00 PM
Venue: Grade 3 room

**Agenda**
1. Funds for the Science Fair.
2. Attendance in October.

**Attendees.** 27 of 40 parents and 1 teacher were present. Quorum was met.

**Proceedings.**
1. The adviser said the Science Fair needs materials for posters. A parent asked how much each learner will give.
2. The adviser reported 5 absences in the last week and asked parents to send a note when a child is absent.

| Issues and Concerns | Suggestions | Agreement |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of poster materials | Share materials among groups | Groups will share; amount to be set by the HPTA officers |
| Absences | Parents send a text to the adviser | Parents will text the adviser on the day of absence |

**Adjournment.** The meeting ended at 4:10 PM.

Prepared by: ____________________ Teacher A, Secretary
Noted by: ____________________ School Head
