# Incident Report: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A factual record of one incident, such as a fight, bullying or an injury, that the teacher saw or was told about. It goes to the School Head and the guidance designate. It is confidential. The form carries a data privacy notice.

## Page setup
A4, portrait, Arial 12, 1-inch margins. Identifying information is a three-box table. The privacy notice sits at the bottom of the last page.

## Parts, in order
| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| INCIDENT REPORT FORM | Fixed title | Code | One line |
| Type of incident | Short label (for example: bullying, fight, injury) | Teacher | 1 to 3 words |
| Date, time, and location of the incident | When and where | Teacher | One line |
| II. Identifying Information, A. Alleged Victim | Name (Optional), Age, Gender, Grade Level, Status | Teacher; blank by default | Fields blank |
| B. Alleged Perpetrator | Name (Optional), Age, Gender, Grade Level, Status | Teacher; blank by default | Fields blank |
| C. Complainant | Name (Optional), Relationship to the alleged victim | Teacher; blank by default | Fields blank |
| Specific details of the incident (What, Where, When, How, Why/possible cause/s) | The facts in order | Teacher facts, AI wording | 5 to 10 sentences |
| Action taken by the school | What was done and who was told | Teacher facts, AI wording | Short list with times |
| Prepared by | Name, position/designation, signature | Teacher, blank | Line empty |
| Reviewed by | School LRP Focal/Guidance Counselor/Teacher | Blank | Line empty |
| Noted by | Principal | Blank | Line empty |
| Date Reported | Date filed | Teacher | One line |
| Data Privacy Notice | Fixed notice text | Code | Small print |

## How to fill each part
- **Type of incident.** Plain label. Good: "Physical altercation". Bad: "Bullying case of a problem child" (label on a person).
- **Date, time, and location.** Exact. Good: "October 20, 2026, 10:15 AM, canal behind the Grade 5 room". Bad: "around recess".
- **Identifying information.** Learner identity stays blank or optional. Names are optional on the form. The AI writes roles ("Learner A") in the narrative; the teacher adds names by hand on the printed form.
- **Specific details.** What a camera would record, in time order. Good: "Learner A took Learner B's bag and threw it into the canal. Learner B cried." Bad: "A is a bully and B provoked it." (label and opinion)
- **Why/possible cause/s.** Write only what the teacher or a witness said, labelled as said. Good: "Learner A said they had argued the day before." Bad: "Because A is jealous."
- **Action taken by the school.** Each action with time and person told. Good: "Separated the learners, 10:20 AM. Informed the School Head, 10:40 AM." If a time is missing, leave a blank to fill.

## Writing rules
- Objective, past tense, no judgment words, no diagnoses, no motives guessed.
- English or Filipino as the teacher wrote. Exact words in quotes in the language used.
- One incident per form.
- Do not recommend punishment or decide guilt.
- Avoid: "bully", "aggressive", "troublemaker", "ADHD", "depressed".

## Free checks code can run
- Date, time and location are filled and the date is not in the future.
- Details have What, Where, When, How parts present or marked "not given".
- No full learner name appears in the AI text (roles only).
- Action list has a time on each line or a blank marker.
- Signature and Date Reported lines are blank.
- Privacy notice is present.

## AI must never write
Names, ages, injuries beyond what the teacher stated, statements not given, causes, blame, diagnoses, dates, times, counts, signatories, sanctions, or anything the teacher did not give. Leave the line blank for the teacher. Learner identity fields stay blank.
