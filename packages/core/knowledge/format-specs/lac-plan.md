# Five-Month LAC Plan: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A proposal for a five-session Learning Action Cell, a school-based professional development group. The LAC leader or focal person files it at the start of the five months. It names the topics, dates, speakers, budget and how results will be checked.

## Page setup
A4 portrait for the profile and signature; the Content Details and M&E tables may be set landscape. 1-inch margins, Arial 12 (tables Arial 11). Form instruction: write N/A if not applicable; do not abbreviate.

## Parts, in order

| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAC PROPONENT PROFILE: School, School ID, Department (Learning Area), School Telephone No., School Email Address, Focal person, Email Address, Mobile No. | Contact details | Profile/letterhead; teacher for focal person, mobile | Exact values, N/A if none |
| LAC CONTENT PROFILE: Title | Name of the LAC program | Teacher; AI suggests | One line |
| Rationale | Reasons, with data on teacher needs, research, legal basis | Teacher facts; AI organizes | One paragraph |
| Program Description | Short description plus Terminal, Application and Results objectives | AI drafts; teacher approves | 2-3 sentences; 3 objectives |
| Focus Professional Standard | Professional Standard, Domain/s, Strand/s, Indicator/s | Teacher | Exact codes as given |
| Target Participants | Participants Profile; total number with sex and age disaggregation | Teacher | Counts that add up |
| Delivery Platform | Face-to-face, online or blended | Teacher | One line |
| Indicative Period of Implementation | Month range | Teacher | e.g. August - December 2026 |
| LAC CONTENT DETAILS table (rows LAC 1 to LAC 5): Date of Conduct & Duration \| Topic \| Session Objectives \| Methodology \| Assessment Strategies & Tools \| Outputs \| Resource Speaker/Subject-Matter Expert | Plan per session | Teacher dates and speakers; AI drafts the rest | Phrases in cells |
| BUDGET ESTIMATE PLAN: Funding Source, Budget Requirements | Who pays; items and amounts | Teacher; total by code | Items with amounts |
| MONITORING AND EVALUATION PLAN table (rows Results, Behavior, Learning, Reaction): Objectives \| Methods and Tools \| Data Sources \| Schedule of M&E \| Person/s Responsible \| Support Needed \| User of M&E Data | Kirkpatrick-model checks | AI drafts; teacher approves | Phrases in cells |
| Declaration: Program Proponent, Signature, Date | Statement and signature | Profile for name; signature and date blank | Blank lines |

## How to fill each part
- Title: Good: "Making Science Lessons Hands-On: A Five-Month LAC". Bad: "LAC".
- Rationale: need, data, basis. Good: "Grade 5 Science MPS was 68%; 6 of 8 teachers asked for practice with hands-on activities." Bad: invented survey figures.
- Objectives: Terminal (by the end of the five months), Application (back in the classroom), Results (as a result of improved practice). Use "will be able to".
- Professional Standard: write the standard, domain, strand and indicator exactly as the teacher gave. Never guess codes.
- Target Participants: Good: "8 Grade 4-6 Science teachers; 6 female, 2 male; ages 25-34: 3, 35-44: 3, 45-54: 2." Bad: counts that do not total.
- LAC rows: one session per row, one small topic each, an output the group can show. Bad: "Discussion" as an output.
- M&E: Results = what changes for learners or school; Behavior = practice in class; Learning = what teachers know; Reaction = how they felt. Do not claim learner gains caused by the LAC.

## Writing rules
- Plain English; future tense; no abbreviations (spell out the full term, as the form says).
- Five sessions, one row each, dates in order.
- Cells are short phrases; the Rationale is the only paragraph.
- Avoid: "etc.", "various", "as needed".

## Free checks code can run
- Exactly 5 LAC rows with dates in order, inside the indicative period and the school year.
- Participant total equals the sex counts and equals the age counts.
- Budget total equals the sum of items; a non-zero budget names a funding source.
- The four M&E rows exist in order: Results, Behavior, Learning, Reaction.
- No empty cell: filled or "N/A".
- Signature and date lines blank.

## AI must never write
- Names of focal person, speakers or participants; school ID; phone, email or mobile numbers.
- Dates, counts, amounts, funding sources, standards or indicator codes the teacher did not give.
- A signature, a date of signing, or claims that the declaration is true.
