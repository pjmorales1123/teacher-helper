# LAC Engagement Report: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
Each LAC member fills this short form at the end of every LAC session. It records how far the member agrees with statements about learning in the session, and four short answers about what went well and what is needed next. It is the member's own voice, in the first person.

## Page setup
A4 portrait, 1-inch margins, Arial 12, table text Arial 11. One page, two at most.

## Parts, in order

| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header: Name of Member, LAC Session (title), Region, Division, Number of LAC Session, Date of LAC Session | Identifies the member and session | Profile and the session record | Blank lines for the teacher; session title if known |
| Part A: agree scale (SD, D, N, A, SA) with Comments/Remarks | Seven indicators about learning, participation and plans to apply | Teacher ticks; code prints the fixed indicators | One tick per row; comment only where the member disagrees |
| Part B 1: The best part of the session | What went well | Teacher notes; AI tidies | 1-3 sentences |
| Part B 2: Further clarification or resources needed | Topics the member still needs help with | Teacher notes; AI tidies | A short list |
| Part B 3: Problems or challenges encountered | What got in the way | Teacher notes; AI tidies | 1-3 sentences |
| Part B 4: Other comments or suggestions | Anything else | Teacher notes; AI tidies | 1-2 sentences |

The Part A indicators are fixed text (for example "I learned a lot from my colleagues in this LAC session"). Code prints them. AI never edits them and never ticks a box.

## How to fill each part
- **Part A.** The member decides every tick. If the teacher gave no ratings, leave the boxes empty. Comments are only for SD or D answers: say why in one line.
- **Best part.** Say what happened, not a feeling alone. Good: "Sharing lesson exemplars helped me see how colleagues pace the Pre-Lesson." Bad: "It was good."
- **Clarification.** Name the topic. Good: "I need examples of rubrics for oral tasks." Bad: "More help."
- **Problems.** Say the problem and, if the teacher gave it, how it was handled. Do not blame a person.
- **Comments.** Keep to suggestions the teacher gave.

## Writing rules
- First person, past tense for what happened, plain English or Filipino as the teacher writes.
- Short answers; no filler praise.
- No judgments about colleagues or the facilitator.

## Free checks code can run
- Every Part A row has at most one tick, or none.
- A comment appears for every SD or D tick (warn, not block).
- Part B items 1 to 4 each have text or are left blank on purpose.
- Header lines present as blanks or filled.

## AI must never write
- A tick on any Part A row.
- Names of the member, facilitator or colleagues.
- Dates, session numbers or titles the teacher did not give.
- Praise or problems the teacher did not state.

**Part B**

3. Problems or challenges encountered: The session started late, so we had less time to discuss.
4. Other comments or suggestions: Please send the session guide one day before.

## Open points
- The full list of Part A indicators is on the form; code must carry them exactly as printed. Confirm the final wording against the current DepEd file.
- Whether the report may be pre-filled from voice notes without ticks is an owner decision.
