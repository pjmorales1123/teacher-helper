# Activity Narrative Report: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A short account of one activity the teacher joined or ran: a training, LAC session, INSET, contest or school event. It is filed with proof of attendance and photos. Participants use it to share what they learned.

## Page setup
A4, portrait, Arial 12, 1-inch margins. Each part is a labelled heading or box followed by text.

## Parts, in order
| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACTIVITY NARRATIVE REPORT | Fixed title | Code | One line |
| Activity Title | Name of the activity | Teacher | One line |
| Organized by | Office or group that ran it | Teacher | One line |
| Date/s of Activity | Dates | Teacher | One line |
| Venue/Location | Place or platform | Teacher | One line |
| Brief Description and Background of the Activity | Why it was held and what it was | Teacher facts, AI wording | 3 to 5 sentences |
| Activity Objectives | What it aimed to do | Teacher or program | 2 to 4 bullets |
| Expected Outputs of the Activity | What was to be produced | Teacher | 1 to 3 bullets |
| Highlights of the Activity | Salient points, agreements, issues, takeaways | Teacher notes, AI wording | 4 to 8 sentences |
| Recommendations | How it helps the school; the participant's commitments | Teacher facts, AI wording | 2 to 4 bullets |
| Action Items | Ways forward to share the learnings | Teacher facts, AI wording | 2 to 4 bullets |
| Attachments: Documentation | Program, photos, certificates, attendance | Teacher | List of names only |
| Prepared by | Participant name and designation | Teacher, blank | Signature line empty |
| Noted by | Head of office name and position | Teacher, blank | Signature line empty |

## How to fill each part
- **Brief Description and Background.** Say what it was, who held it and why. Good: "The one-day LAC on phonics was held to help Grade 2 teachers plan reading remediation." Bad: "It was a wonderful event." (opinion, no fact)
- **Objectives and Expected Outputs.** Use the program's own list if the teacher has it. Never make up objectives.
- **Highlights.** Report what was said and decided, in order. Good: "The speaker showed three ways to teach blending. Teachers agreed to try one in November." Bad: "Everyone learned a lot." (not checkable)
- **Recommendations.** Tie them to the school. Good: "Share the word cards with other Grade 2 teachers."
- **Action Items.** Concrete next steps, each starting with a verb. No dates unless given.
- **Attachments.** List names only, for example "Program, photos, attendance sheet."

## Writing rules
- One voice throughout. Past tense for what happened; present or future for recommendations.
- English or Filipino as the teacher wrote.
- Facts and quotes only from the teacher's notes.
- Avoid: "amazing", "very successful", "fruitful" unless the teacher said so.

## Free checks code can run
- Title, dates and venue are filled.
- Dates are real and not in the future.
- Objectives and Highlights are not empty.
- Highlights have at least 3 sentences when the teacher gave notes.
- Attachments list is filled or says "None".
- Prepared by and Noted by lines are blank.

## AI must never write
Names of speakers or participants, dates, venue, number of participants, attendance, certificates earned, quotations, scores or results, amounts, signatories, and anything not in the teacher's notes. Leave the line blank for the teacher.

## Sample (fictional, for style only)
**ACTIVITY NARRATIVE REPORT**

| Part | Entry |
|---|---|
| Activity Title | LAC Session on Phonics for Grade 2 |
| Organized by | Sample Elementary School |
| Date/s of Activity | November 4, 2026 |
| Venue/Location | School library |

**Brief Description and Background of the Activity.** The session was held to help Grade 2 teachers plan reading remediation. It followed the low reading results in the first term.

**Activity Objectives.**
- Review sound-blending steps.
- Plan one remedial lesson per teacher.

**Expected Outputs of the Activity.** One remedial lesson plan per teacher.

**Highlights of the Activity.** The facilitator showed three ways to teach blending. Teachers practiced each way in pairs. They agreed to try the second way in November. One concern was the lack of printed word cards.

**Recommendations.**
- Share the word cards among Grade 2 sections.
- Hold a follow-up session in December.

**Action Items.**
- Teach the blending lesson in each section.
- Report the results at the next LAC.

**Attachments:** Documentation (program, photos, attendance sheet).

Prepared by: ______________________ Teacher A, Participant
Noted by: ______________________ School Head
