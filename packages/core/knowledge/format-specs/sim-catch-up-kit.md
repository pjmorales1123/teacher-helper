# Strategic Intervention Material (SIM) Catch-up Kit: how to write it

## What it is and when a teacher uses it
A SIM is a small kit of cards that helps learners master one skill they missed. A teacher uses it for learners who did not reach mastery on a competency. It has six cards. The app adds a learner-group page so the teacher can assign the kit.

## Page setup
- A4, Arial 12, 1-inch margins.
- Cover page first, then one card per page, with the card name as the heading.
- The Answer Key is a separate last page the teacher can remove before copying.
- Learner-facing cards use short lines and large spacing for writing.

## Parts, in order
| Part | What goes in it | Who supplies it | Length and style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover | School, "Strategic Intervention Material", subject, topic, grade, author, date | Profile, teacher | Facts only |
| Guide Card | Competency, how to use the kit, steps in order, time | AI; competency from picker | Half page, numbered steps |
| Activity Card | A practice task that builds the skill | AI | 1 task, 5 to 10 minutes |
| Assessment Card | 5 to 10 items on the same skill | AI | Numbered items, one answer each |
| Enrichment Card | A reflection or extension task | AI | 2 to 3 prompts or a sentence frame |
| Reference Card | Short notes and a worked example of the skill | AI | Half page, plain words |
| Answer Key | Answers to the Activity and Assessment cards | Code from AI items | List by number |
| Learner groups (app addition) | Names of learners per group, support level, card assigned | Teacher | Table, teacher fills the names |

## How to fill each part
### Cover
Facts from the profile and teacher. Never invented.

### Guide Card
State the competency exactly as the picker gives it. List the steps: read the Reference Card, do the Activity Card, answer the Assessment Card, check with the key, do the Enrichment Card.
- Pattern: "Step 1. Read the Reference Card. Step 2. Do the Activity Card."
- Bad: "Do all the cards." It gives no order.

### Activity Card
One task that practices the skill with support. Include the directions and room to write.
- Pattern: "Find the area of each triangle. Use A = 1/2 x b x h. Show your work."
- Bad: "Practice triangles." No task.

### Assessment Card
Items are on the competency only. One correct answer each. Easy items first.
- Pattern: "A triangle has a base of 6 cm and a height of 4 cm. What is its area?"
- Bad: a trick question about the wording of the topic. Items test the skill, not the reading of the title.

### Enrichment Card
A reflection or an extension for learners who finish.
- Pattern: "I understand that ___. I realized that ___."

### Reference Card
Short notes, one worked example, one memory tip.

### Answer Key
Numbered answers that match the cards exactly. Code builds it from the item answers, not from a second AI pass.

### Learner groups (app addition)
A table with columns Group, Learners, Support level, Card to start with. The teacher types the names. AI leaves it blank.

## Writing rules
- One language, English or Filipino, as the teacher chooses.
- Reading level at or one grade below the learners' grade.
- Short sentences. One direction per sentence.
- Present tense for directions: "Find", "Write", "Circle".
- Keep each card to one page.
- One skill per kit. If two skills are needed, make two kits.
- Avoid: "etc.", "and so on", vague directions, and items with two correct answers.

## Free checks code can run
- All six cards are present and in order.
- Assessment has 5 to 10 items. Each item has exactly one answer.
- Every item and activity answer appears in the Answer Key by number.
- Competency text equals the picker text.
- Guide Card steps match the card names.
- No placeholder text.
- Learner names appear only in the groups table and only if the teacher typed them.

## AI must never write
- Names of learners, teachers or the school, dates, signatories.
- Competency text or standards.
- Learner groups or any statement about a learner's ability.
- Any fact about the school.
- Questions about the wording of the topic itself.

## Sample (fictional, for style only)
Grade 6 Mathematics, topic: area of a triangle. School and author are placeholders.

**Cover:** [School Name] | Strategic Intervention Material | Mathematics 6 | Area of a Triangle | Author: [Teacher Name] | [Date]

**Guide Card**

| Item | Entry |
|---|---|
| Competency | [Copied from the curriculum picker] |
| Time | 30 minutes |
| Steps | 1. Read the Reference Card. 2. Do the Activity Card. 3. Answer the Assessment Card. 4. Check with the Answer Key. 5. Do the Enrichment Card. |

**Reference Card:** The area of a triangle is half of base times height. A = 1/2 x b x h. Example: base 8 cm, height 5 cm. 8 x 5 = 40. Half of 40 is 20. The area is 20 square centimeters (cm2). Tip: the height makes a square corner with the base.

**Activity Card:** Find the area. Show your work.

| No. | Base | Height | Area |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 cm | 4 cm | |
| 2 | 10 cm | 3 cm | |
| 3 | 7 cm | 6 cm | |

**Assessment Card:** Write the answer with units.
1. A triangle has base 12 cm and height 5 cm. What is its area?
2. A triangle has base 9 cm and height 4 cm. What is its area?
3. A triangular flag has base 20 cm and height 10 cm. What is its area?
4. A triangle has base 14 cm and height 6 cm. What is its area?
5. A triangle has an area of 24 cm2 and a base of 8 cm. What is its height?

**Enrichment Card:** I understand that ___. I realized that ___. Draw a triangle on grid paper and find its area.

|---|---|---|---|
| A | [Teacher to type names] | Needs help with the formula | Reference Card |

**Answer Key**

## Open points
- DM 117 s.2005 is the research basis for the six cards; the signed memo was not read. Marked unverified.
- The Answer Key is a separate sheet in the sample SIM; the app lists it as the sixth card.
- The learner-group table is an app addition. Keep it removable so the kit prints without names.
- The Cainta sample was SHS Earth Science; checks for other grades are by this spec only.
