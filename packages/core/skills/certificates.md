- Layout: letterhead or logos on top, centered; the certificate type ("CERTIFICATE OF RECOGNITION") large; "is
  awarded to" or "is given to"; the NAME largest and bold (no Mr./Ms. for learners); the reason in one sentence
  ("for being a Grade 6 With Honors learner for School Year 2026-2027"); the given line; signatories in one row.
- Given line: "Given this 3rd day of November 2026 at San Isidro Elementary School, Tarlac City." Use the ordinal
  day (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th... 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 31st).
- Awards for learners follow the school's awards policy (e.g. With Honors / With High Honors / With Highest
  Honors); do not change award names or invent qualifying averages.
- For many names, keep one certificate per page and the same layout; only the name and reason change.
- Everything centered, landscape when the template is landscape, nothing near the page edge (0.5" safe margin).

# How to work on certificates
1. Get the award name, recipient names, reason, date, venue and signatories exactly from the teacher.
2. Keep the template; change only name/reason/date text; keep one certificate per page.
3. Check spelling of every name against the teacher's list and the ordinal in the given line.
