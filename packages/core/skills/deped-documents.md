# Use real structure, not manual formatting
- Headings use Heading 1 / Heading 2 / Heading 3 styles (never bold Normal text as a fake heading).
  Keep one level per depth: main sections Heading 1, subsections Heading 2.
- Document title uses the Title style, once.
- Lists use list formatting (op paragraph with list) or List Bullet / List Number styles, not typed dashes.
- Tables get a header row with short, clear column names; keep one fact per cell.
- One font family for body text. Do not mix fonts inside a section.
- No empty paragraphs for spacing and no double spaces between sentences.
- Line spacing: 1.0 to 1.15 for forms and lesson plans; research papers follow their template.

# DepEd Manual of Style (DMOS)
- DepEd adopted the DepEd Manual of Style (2018 edition) through DO 30, s. 2019. It is largely based on
  the Chicago Manual of Style, 17th edition.
- A 2022 DepEd memorandum reiterating the DMOS sets Bookman Old Style for issuances, letters and
  official communications (reported size 11 pt; confirm with the division template), and requires the
  block letter format for letters.
- Use Bookman Old Style only for official issuances, letters and memos, or when the teacher asks.
  For lesson plans and class documents, keep the font the template already uses.
- Page size: follow the template. Use A4 when the teacher asks for DepEd standard paper and nothing else
  is specified; mention that some offices still require Letter or Legal.
- Dates in official letters: Month Day, Year (e.g. November 3, 2026). Spell out the school name in full.

# Research papers (action research, DO 16, s. 2017)
Action research report outline: Title Page, Abstract, Acknowledgment, Context and Rationale,
Innovation/Intervention/Strategy, Action Research Questions, Action Research Methods
(participants and/or other sources of data, data gathering methods, data analysis plan),
Discussion of Results and Reflection, Action Research Work Plan and Timelines, Cost Estimates,
Plans for Dissemination and Utilization, References, Appendices.
Proposals use: Context and Rationale, Action Research Questions, Proposed Innovation,
Action Research Methods, Work Plan, Cost Estimates, Plans for Dissemination and Advocacy, References.
- Cite with APA 7th edition unless the division says otherwise. Never invent sources:
  write [CITATION NEEDED] where a source is required.
