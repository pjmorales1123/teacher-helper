# Component weights (Written Work / Performance Task / Examination)
- English, Filipino, Mathematics, Science, Araling Panlipunan, GMRC/VE: 20 / 50 / 30.
- MAPEH and EPP/TLE: 20 / 60 / 20.
- SHS (Table 10): core and academic electives 20/50/30; field exposure, arts apprenticeship, creative production
  15/70/15; arts, sports, health and wellness 20/60/20; research and design 40/60/0; TechPro 15/65/20;
  work immersion 20/80/0. Grade 12 in SY 2026-2027 still uses DO 8, s. 2015 weights: keep the workbook's.
- Within Examination: short test 1 = 30%, short test 2 = 30%, term exam = 40% (Section 45).
- Formative assessment is not counted. Extra points are teacher-approved additions to a Performance Task item.
- Three terms per school year; the final grade is the mean of the term grades, rounded to a whole number;
  75 and above is passed.

- The minimum reportable transmuted grade is 60. From SY 2027-2028 grading is zero-based: no table, the term
  grade is the initial grade.
- Not stated in the order (say so if asked, do not decide it): rounding the initial grade before lookup, and
  exact-half rounding. Keep whatever the workbook does.
- In a workbook, build the lookup as a formula (VLOOKUP/LOOKUP against a table on its own sheet), never typed grades.

# Descriptors (Table 11, numerical grades)
90-100 Advancing (Namumukod-tangi); 80-89 Benchmarking (Napamamalas); 75-79 Connecting (Natutungo);
65-74 Developing (Napauunlad); 0-64 Emerging (Nagsisimula).
Kindergarten uses CO Consistent, DV Developing, BG Beginning (no numbers for parents); Grade 1 uses letters
A Advancing to E Emerging. The old DO 8, s. 2015 descriptors (Outstanding, Very Satisfactory, ...) are replaced.
