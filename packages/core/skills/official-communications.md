# Letters (block format, DMOS)
Parts in order, all flush left: letterhead (Republic of the Philippines / Department of Education / Region /
Schools Division Office / school name, address, contact), date (November 3, 2026), inside address (NAME IN BOLD CAPS,
position, office, address), optional Thru: line for letters routed through the principal or division,
salutation ("Dear Dr. Santos:" or "Dear Sir/Madam:"), body, complimentary close ("Very truly yours," or
"Respectfully yours,"), signature block (NAME IN BOLD CAPS, position), then enclosures/cc if any.
- First paragraph states the purpose; then details (what, when, where, who); the last paragraph states the
  requested action and thanks. Short paragraphs, no slang, no exclamation points.
- Requests to the SDS go through the principal and, for some matters, the district supervisor (Thru: lines).

# Memoranda (school/division memo)
- Heading: MEMORANDUM (or "School Memorandum No. __, s. 2026"), then TO, FROM, SUBJECT, DATE lines aligned.
  SUBJECT in bold caps, specific (e.g. "CONDUCT OF THE FIRST TERM SCHOOL-BASED SCIENCE FAIR").
- Body as numbered paragraphs: purpose and legal basis (cite only orders the teacher gave or that you verified),
  details (date, venue, participants, requirements), funding ("subject to the usual accounting and auditing rules"),
  contact person, and the closing line "Immediate dissemination of and compliance with this Memorandum is desired."
- Enclosures are listed and referred to as "Enclosure No. 1".

# Endorsements
- 1st Indorsement / 2nd Indorsement format: date, routing line, numbered body, signature. Keep the original letter's
  reference (subject, date) in the first line.

# How to work on a letter or memo
1. Identify the sender, recipient (name, position, office), purpose and routing (Thru:) from the request and files.
2. Keep the school's letterhead and signature blocks; fill names and positions only from the teacher's data.
3. Purpose first, details second, action and thanks last. One page when possible.
4. Check: date format, names in bold caps in address and signature, correct salutation punctuation (colon),
   enclosures listed if mentioned, no invented order numbers.
