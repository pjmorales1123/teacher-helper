# Activity proposal / activity design
Order: Title of the activity; Proponent(s); Rationale (the need, with data the teacher gave and the DepEd policy or
program it supports, only if verified); Objectives (general, then specific and measurable); Target participants and
number; Date, time and venue; Methodology or program of activities (a matrix: time, activity, person responsible);
Budgetary requirements (item, quantity, unit cost, total, with the source of funds such as MOOE, SEF, PTA or
donations; totals must add up); Expected outputs/outcomes; Monitoring and evaluation; signatories
(Prepared by / Recommending approval / Approved by).

# Narrative report
Title; date and venue; participants (number and groups); objectives; highlights or proceedings in past tense, in
the order they happened; outcomes and insights; recommendations; documentation (photos with captions
"Figure 1. ..." under each); Prepared by / Noted by. Report only what the teacher told you or what the files show.

# Accomplishment report
There is no national accomplishment-report template: follow the school's or division's format if the document has one.
A table per period: program/project/activity, objectives, date, persons involved, accomplishments (measurable:
numbers, percentages), remarks or MOVs (means of verification). Past tense, quantified, no adjectives as evidence.

# Minutes of meeting
Header (school, "Minutes of the ___ Meeting"), date, time started, venue, presiding officer, attendees present and
absent (or "see attached attendance"), call to order, prayer/opening, approval of the previous minutes, matters
discussed (one heading per agenda item: discussion summary, agreements, action points with person responsible and
deadline), other matters, adjournment time, Prepared by (secretary), Noted by (principal). Summarize; do not
quote people unless asked.

# All reports
- Use the school's full name, the correct school year (SY 2026-2027) and term/quarter.
- Numbers in tables right-aligned; totals checked. Peso amounts ₱1,250.00.

# How to work on a proposal or report
1. Get the facts from the teacher: activity, dates, venue, participants, budget lines, results, photos.
2. Keep the school's template; fill every part; leave [DATA: ...] where facts are missing.
3. Check numbers: budget totals, participant counts, percentages, dates within the school year.
