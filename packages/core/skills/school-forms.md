- SF1 School Register; SF2 Daily Attendance Report of Learners; SF3 Books Issued and Returned; SF4 Monthly Learner's
  Movement and Attendance; SF5 Report on Promotion and Level of Proficiency; SF6 Summarized Report on Promotion;
  SF7 School Personnel Assignment List; SF8 Learner's Basic Health and Nutrition Report; SF9 Learner's Progress
  Report Card (formerly Form 138); SF10 Learner's Permanent Academic Record (formerly Form 137).
- Official school forms come only from DepEd LIS; DepEd treats commercial electronic school forms as null and void.
  Work only on a form the teacher downloaded or opened, never recreate an official form from scratch.
- DO 006, s. 2025 (streamlining of school forms, Enclosure 1) names five core forms: Learning Outcome Assessment,
  Class Record, Test Result and Analysis, Report on Remedial and Intervention Program, and Individualized Learning
  Plan. Lesson plans, SF2, SF9 and SF10 are not among those five.
- SF2 legend (verified on the LIS senior high form): blank = present, x = absent, shaded upper half = late (tardy),
  lower half = cutting class. Monthly totals of absences and tardiness; five consecutive absences need an
  intervention note. The elementary/JHS legend may differ: follow the form's own legend.
- These forms are generated or checked against LIS; keep their exact layout, column order, codes and legends.
  Only fill cells; never add, remove or rename columns.
- Learner names: LAST NAME, First Name Middle Name (as in the LRN record); males listed first, then females, each
  alphabetical, when the form groups by sex.
- SF2: mark absences and tardiness only with the form's own symbols; totals per learner and per day must match.
- LRN is 12 digits; keep it as text so leading zeros are not lost (numberFormat "@" / plain text).
