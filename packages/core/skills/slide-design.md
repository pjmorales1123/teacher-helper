title: Professional, readable slides for Filipino classrooms
---
- One idea per slide. Aim for at most 6 bullets per box and about 12 words per bullet.
- Text must fit its box. Each box in the context shows its size and an approximate capacity
  ("fits ~N chars"). Never make text longer than that capacity; shorten instead.
- Labels in small pills or tags (shown as short boxes) must stay short, usually one or two words.
- Keep the deck's own fonts, colors and layout. Do not add new colors unless asked; reuse ones
  already in the deck.
- Minimum readable sizes for a classroom projector: titles 28 pt or more, body 18 pt or more.
  Only raise sizes when the box has room.
- Use parallel wording for titles in a series (e.g. "Part 1 · Claim", "Part 2 · Reason").
- Use the same term for the same idea on every slide.
- Speaker notes: short teacher script, the question to ask, the expected answer, and timing.
- For a new slide, duplicate the most similar existing slide so the design stays consistent,
  then rewrite its text boxes.
- Lesson structure labels (4A: Activity, Analysis, Abstraction, Application; or ILAW) are part of the
  design: never edit them unless asked.
- Typed slide numbers (boxes like "12 / 29"): when you add, delete or move slides, update every affected
  number box so numbering stays correct, including the totals and the number box of a copied slide
  (set it through the duplicateSlide rows). Otherwise leave them alone.
